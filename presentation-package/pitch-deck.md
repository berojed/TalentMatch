SLIDE 1 — TITLE
Product: TalentMatch
Tagline: Match students to the right research opportunities faster.
Visual cue: slide1_landing.png

SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM
Headline: Great candidates get lost in manual matching
Body (≤3 bullets, ≤8 words each):
- Reviews happen one application at a time.
- Skill fit is hard to compare quickly.
- Students wait without clear progress.

SLIDE 3 — THE SOLUTION
Headline: One platform for both sides of matching
Body (≤2 sentences, ≤30 words total):
TalentMatch gives students and supervisors focused workspaces. Students apply with structured profiles, while supervisors review candidates faster with clear status actions and visible fit signals.

SLIDE 4 — KEY FEATURES (max 5)
- Role-based portals: Show only each role’s tasks.
- Structured profiles: Compare applicants on consistent inputs.
- Opportunity browse: Filter, list, or swipe quickly.
- Application timeline: Track each stage after submission.
- Review workspace: Decide faster with fit signals.

SLIDE 5 — STUDENT FLOW
Steps (4–5):
1. Sign up and choose the Student role.
2. Browse projects in list or swipe view.
3. Open details and submit an application.
4. Track status from review to decision.
Visual cue: slide5_student_opportunities.png

SLIDE 6 — SUPERVISOR FLOW
Steps (4–5):
1. Sign up and choose the Supervisor role.
2. Post opportunities with level and requirements.
3. Review candidates in list or swipe mode.
4. Open full application and make a decision.
Visual cue: slide6_supervisor_applications.png

SLIDE 7 — HOW MATCHING WORKS
Plain explanation (≤40 words):
Today, matching is transparent and rule-based. It combines profile signals like skills, experience, projects, and academic completeness into an illustrative score and breakdown. A trained model is planned next.
