SLIDE 1
TalentMatch connects students and supervisors around real research opportunities. The product goal is simple: reduce manual matching work and help both sides reach good-fit decisions faster. This opening screen sets that promise in plain language.

SLIDE 2
Today, matching often depends on slow, manual review. Supervisors sort many applications one by one, comparisons are inconsistent, and students cannot clearly see where they stand. The result is delay, uncertainty, and avoidable drop-off on both sides.

SLIDE 3
TalentMatch structures the process into clear steps for each role. Students submit complete profiles and apply directly. Supervisors review from one workspace with status actions and visible fit signals. This improves speed while keeping decisions understandable.

SLIDE 4
These five features are the core product surface. They turn matching from scattered messages and files into one guided flow. The emphasis is practical: easier comparison, faster triage, and clearer progress tracking for everyone involved.

SLIDE 5
For students, the path is direct: choose role, browse opportunities, apply, and track progress. The opportunity page supports both scanning and quick decisions, while the timeline view gives transparency after submission instead of silence.

SLIDE 6
For supervisors, the workflow centers on control and speed. They can post opportunities, review applicants in different modes, and open a full review before deciding. The goal is less administrative overhead without removing human judgment.

SLIDE 7
The score shown today is rule-based, not a trained model. It uses visible profile signals and shows a breakdown so supervisors can understand what drives the result. This keeps the system transparent now, while leaving room for a stronger model later.

SLIDE 8
Most core flows are already built and live. Students can navigate the full application cycle, and supervisors can review with decision controls. The remaining roadmap items are finishing project posting/editing and adding an admin workspace.

SLIDE 9
Value is role-specific: students get faster discovery and clearer progress, supervisors spend less time on manual triage, and institutions gain more consistent placement outcomes. TalentMatch is designed to make research matching fairer, faster, and easier to run.
